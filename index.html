02About
portrait — shruti, natural lightthat's me

I'm a designer who asks “why” one more time than is comfortable — then sweats the pixels until the answer feels obvious.

For six years I've worked on products where the stakes are real: money, health, getting home on time. I like problems that look complicated from the outside, and I like sitting with people until I see them from the inside.

I studied communication design at NID, then fell for interaction design through a summer of usability tests at a fintech startup. I've been translating between engineers, researchers and users ever since.

If a user has to admire the interface, it's in the way. I'd rather design something that disappears into the task, and save the delight for the moments that have earned it.

Designing for the tired, the distracted, the first-timer and the screen-reader user usually produces a better product for everyone. Accessibility is where I start, not something I check at the end.

Rough prototypes in front of real people beat polished decks in front of stakeholders. I build early, test often and stay comfortable being wrong in week two.

A good design system is a gift to your future self and to every engineer who touches the code. I document decisions, not just components.
